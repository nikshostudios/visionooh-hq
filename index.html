Where do these skills come from?

Claude wrote all eleven, from your business profile. I gave it the structure from a kit I built before and the official skill format, then checked each one for outdoor work. So they’re made for VisionOOH, not copied from somewhere.

If you want to see where the idea comes from, there are two official places:

  • github.com/anthropics/skills Anthropic’s own example skills, like the Word, Excel, PDF and skill-creator ones. Many already come with your paid plan.
  • agentskills.io The open format a skill follows. Any skill made this way works in Claude.
